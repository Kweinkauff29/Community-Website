BEFORE YOU BUY

THE QUESTIONS
WORTH ASKING.

International real estate can involve additional financial, legal, travel and ownership considerations. Start with the right questions and the right professionals.

ADVISORY PRINCIPLE

EXPERTISE MATTERS

Cross-border transactions involve legal, tax and financial requirements unique to each purchaser. We recommend consulting licensed legal, tax and immigration professionals alongside your CCOR REALTOR®.

01 FINANCING

Can an international buyer finance a U.S. property?

Financing may be available to foreign-national buyers. Lender requirements, documentation, down payments and loan terms can differ substantially depending on the institution, credit history, and loan program.

02 TRAVEL + IMMIGRATION

Does buying Florida property give me the right to live in the United States?

No. Real-estate ownership and U.S. immigration status are separate matters. Purchasing real property does not confer residency or visa privileges.

U.S. Department of State Visa Resources
03 TAXES + FIRPTA

Are there special tax considerations for foreign owners?

There can be. International buyers should discuss U.S. and home-country tax considerations with qualified advisers. When a foreign person later disposes of U.S. real property, the Foreign Investment in Real Property Tax Act (FIRPTA) generally creates a federal withholding requirement. Exceptions, exemptions and special withholding rules can apply.

Official IRS FIRPTA Information
04 CONDOMINIUMS

What should I review before buying a Florida condo?

Essential condominium review items include association fees, operating budget and reserve funds, current or proposed special assessments, master property insurance, rental restrictions, Milestone inspection status, and Structural Integrity Reserve Studies (SIRS).

Florida DBPR Condominium Information
05 INSURANCE

What makes Florida property insurance different?

Buyers should investigate property insurance early in the purchase process and understand applicable windstorm, hurricane, flood, deductible and property-condition considerations (such as roof age and 4-point inspection requirements).

06 RENTALS

Can I rent my Florida property while I’m away?

Possibly. Rental rules vary by city or county, community, condominium or homeowners association, property type and intended rental period. Verify rental eligibility before you buy.

07 OWNING FROM ABROAD

Who takes care of the property while I’m away?

Seasonal owners commonly plan for professional property management, home-watch services, landscaping, pool care, utilities, preventive maintenance and storm preparation.

08 OWNERSHIP RULES

Do international buyers face special ownership rules?

Certain federal and Florida requirements may depend on the purchaser, ownership structure and property location. Buyers should obtain qualified legal guidance for their individual circumstances.
