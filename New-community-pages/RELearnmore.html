License first.
Then grow.

In Florida, “sales associate” is the state license. REALTOR® is a membership mark for real-estate professionals who belong to the National Association of REALTORS® and follow its Code of Ethics. These steps focus on initial sales-associate licensure.

01

Confirm eligibility

Start with the requirements.

Applicants must be at least 18, have a high-school diploma or equivalent, and have a U.S. Social Security number. Review the current state checklist before spending money on a course.

Official DBPR checklist →
02

Pre-license education

Complete 63 approved hours.

Complete a Florida Real Estate Commission-approved 63-hour pre-license course. The course completion is generally valid for licensure purposes for two years; confirm exceptions and current provider status with DBPR.

DBPR education resources →
03

Application + screening

Apply and submit fingerprints.

Submit the sales associate application, required fee and electronic fingerprints. The state must approve your application before you can schedule the licensing examination.

DBPR application center →
04

State exam

Schedule and pass the exam.

After authorization, schedule the Florida Real Estate Sales Associate examination with Pearson VUE. A score of 75 or higher is required for the standard sales-associate examination.

Pearson VUE exam page →
05

Activate

Work under a Florida broker.

A new license is issued inactive unless it is activated. To practice, a sales associate works under the direction, control and management of a licensed broker or owner-developer.

Licensure information →
06

First renewal + beyond

Keep learning.

Sales associates generally complete 45 hours of approved post-license education before the first renewal. Later renewal periods generally require 14 hours of continuing education; verify the exact requirement for your license.
